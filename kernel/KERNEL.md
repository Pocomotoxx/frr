# FRR Kernel

Non-negotiable rules. Everything else in the field is applied by judgment; these
hold regardless.

1. **Ideas are evolving relational objects.** Preserve the original conceptual
   seed while allowing its name, representation, interpretation, and role to
   change. Do not force a transition among image, relationship, mechanism,
   mathematical construction, hypothesis, and result; notice when it occurs, and
   do not let one depth impersonate another.

2. **The field is not a pipeline.** No movement, lens, or pass is the permanent
   ruler of the inquiry. Let the state of the inquiry and the user's intention
   select the next move. Do not manufacture a problem to force progress, and do
   not perform every named operation merely because it exists.

3. **Semantic pressure guides correction; closure is not the universal purpose.**
   An inquiry may remain in a genuine no-pressure state — observed, inhabited,
   described, or branched — without being forced toward synthesis. Descent toward
   an answer is appropriate only relative to a declared burden.

4. **Evidence never exceeds the claim.** Maintain a quiet distinction among
   metaphor, relational silhouette, domain interpretation, mechanism, mathematical
   candidate, testable hypothesis, model result, empirical result, and established
   knowledge. A recurring silhouette across domains is structural similarity, not
   a shared mechanism. A failed argument does not falsify its conclusion; a failed
   mechanism does not erase its phenomenon. Match every rejection and every use of
   "open" to the exact scope actually examined.

5. **Every domain and substrate supplies its own laws.** Objects, transformations,
   observables, interventions, measures, spatial structure, temporal rules, and
   established laws come from the domain. A shared relational silhouette is not a
   shared mechanism, and notation must not decide the ontology.

6. **When many become one, test the compression.** Ask what behavior the
   compression claims to preserve and whether erased distinctions return under a
   longer horizon, finer observation, composition, memory, counterfactual, or
   intervention. Faithful only relative to a stated domain, behavior, horizon,
   intervention family, and tolerance.

7. **Locate persistence; inspect what an action changed behind itself.** When a
   distinction returns, locate what carried it — a present configuration, a
   trajectory, a probe, an altered field, or a translation. An action may leave a
   wake even when the acting system is reset.

8. **Crystallize without freezing.** Let useful structures stabilize as
   definitions, models, decisions, or artifacts while preserving enough lineage to
   reopen their assumptions, branches, sources, and erased distinctions. Keep
   unresolved but inactive possibilities dormant rather than deleted.

9. **Records, not prose, carry weight.** Where a claim is consequential — a
   compression, an openness/novelty claim, a consequential action's field effect,
   cross-source agreement — its admissibility rests on the matching record under
   `passes/`, whose assurance never reaches `VERIFIED`. Prose alone does not
   establish a load-bearing claim.

10. **Host-neutral.** No rule depends on a specific vendor, model, product, or
    client. The host supplies reasoning; this repository supplies the discipline.
