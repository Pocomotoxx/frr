# FRR — Fluid Relational Reasoning

**Version:** v0.4 (Residual Location and Field Change)
**Kind:** a Markdown-based, host-neutral reasoning system. A host reads these files
and reasons by them; the repository supplies the discipline, not the vendor.

## Purpose

FRR treats ideas as **evolving relational objects** rather than fixed definitions.
It is a reasoning *field*, not a pipeline: a host moves fluidly among relational
lenses, lets genuine pressure guide correction, tests whether a compression erased
distinctions that later return, locates where persistence is carried, and lets
structures crystallize without freezing or erasing how they formed.

FRR preserves the original conceptual seed while allowing its name, representation,
interpretation, and role to change as it is approached from different directions.

## Two layers

FRR has a **field** and a **records** layer, and they are different in kind.

- The **field** (`kernel/`, `field/`) is advisory reasoning discipline. It is
  read and applied by judgment; it has no mandatory order and produces no forced
  artifact. The field is not a pipeline.
- The **records** (`passes/`, `runtime/schemas/`, `scripts/`) are the small set of
  moments where the field's discipline is made **checkable**: a compression that
  claims fidelity, a claim of openness or novelty, a consequential action's field
  effect, and cross-source agreement each emit an evidence-bound record that a
  validator checks for shape and internal consistency. A record's assurance never
  reaches `VERIFIED`; a passing validator proves the check was posed, not that the
  world agrees.

The field stays fluid; only the load-bearing claims are pinned. This is the
deliberate synthesis: honor FRR's field nature, and still let its consequential
claims be audited.

## Host neutrality

The host is any assistant that can read this repository and reason by its Markdown
rules. No rule, schema, or record may depend on a specific vendor, model, product,
or client. A host supplies reasoning and tools; this repository supplies the
discipline.

## Entry

Read this file, then [BOOT.md](BOOT.md). For the shortest usable form, load only
[field/COMPACT_SEED.md](field/COMPACT_SEED.md).

## Lifecycle

`FRR.md` → `BOOT.md` → `kernel/KERNEL.md` → the field (`field/`) →
the records where claims carry weight (`passes/`) → response.

## Not a promise of final ground

FRR helps an idea acquire enough form for its present purpose without taking away
its ability to continue changing. It does not promise a final answer, and it does
not let prose stand in for evidence.
