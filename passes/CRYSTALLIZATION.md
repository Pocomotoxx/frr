# Pass: Crystallization, Dormancy, and Reopening

**When:** a relationship stabilizes as a definition, model, theorem candidate,
program, decision, explanation, artifact, or shared reference point. Advisory only —
this pass carries no record.

**Principle.** Crystallization is provisional stabilization, not a declaration that
movement has ended. A crystallized object must remain rehydratable.

**Preserve enough lineage to recover** the seed and question it grew from; the
interpretations and assumptions it retained; the branches it merged or excluded;
the evidence and sources that changed it; the compression criterion and distinctions
it erased; the domain, horizon, intervention family, and tolerance within which it
was stabilized; and the strongest unresolved alternatives.

**Branch states.** A branch may be active (it currently changes the inquiry),
dormant (plausible but without present pressure), residual (a useful part survives a
failed larger interpretation), contradicted within stated conditions, or released
(retaining it adds no recoverable value). Dormancy preserves possibility without
letting lineage overwhelm the live field. Contradiction records the conditions that
produced it. Release is not disguised as refutation.

**Run stabilization backward** when needed: from a finished answer, model, or
artifact, ask whether its assumptions, transformations, sources, and erased
distinctions can still be reconstructed. If they cannot, the result may be useful,
but its lineage is not yet trustworthy.
